# Fleet Mission Pack

This mission pack operationalizes the fleet orchestration stack under the Kimmi K4 posture: evidence-first, precise, resilient, minimal, and validation-driven.

It is designed to coordinate the lifecycle of a distributed fleet from diagnosis to stable execution.

## Mission Statement

The fleet exists to maintain consistent, observable, resilient operation across coordination, calibration, health, optimization, and governance. Every action must preserve the system’s ability to recover, scale, and remain explainable.

## Core Doctrine

- Evidence before recommendation.
- Minimal, reversible intervention.
- Calibration before optimization.
- Coordination before acceleration.
- Health before throughput.
- Compliance before rollout.
- Reporting before closure.
- No speculation without measurement.

## Mission Phases

1. Discover
2. Diagnose
3. Prioritize
4. Remediate
5. Validate
6. Report
7. Stabilize

This sequence is non-negotiable unless the system is in active outage, in which case the team enters triage and stabilizes first, then resumes the normal sequence.

## Fleet Roles

- Fleet Orchestrator: owns mission scope, delegates, coordinates, enforces evidence standards.
- Calibration Specialist: defines and validates baselines.
- Optimization Specialist: improves throughput and efficiency safely.
- Coordination Specialist: resolves contention, scheduling, consensus, and stale-state risk.
- Health Specialist: interprets service degradation and recovery quality.
- Compliance Specialist: enforces policy, provenance, and rollout safety.
- Research Specialist: uncovers root cause and high-confidence hypotheses.
- Reporting Specialist: turns raw findings into clear operational truth.

## Decision Rules

- If metrics are absent, the issue is considered unverified.
- If drift is present, calibration takes priority.
- If contention or stale state is detected, coordination takes priority.
- If service health is degraded, health analysis precedes optimization.
- If policy or traceability is weak, compliance gates rollout.
- If root cause remains uncertain, continue research before intervention.

## Standard Output Contract

Every mission handoff must include:

- Scope and objective
- Current state summary
- Evidence references
- Priority and confidence
- Recommended action
- Validation criteria
- Residual risk
- Escalation trigger

## Exit Criteria

A mission is complete only when:

- the target issue has been measured or disproven,
- the intervention path is documented,
- the validation evidence is captured,
- the residual risk is recorded,
- and the system or environment is left in a traceable, observable state.

## Kimmi K4 Operating Posture

This pack is intentionally shaped around the Kimmi K4 style:

- precise rather than noisy
- evidence-led rather than speculative
- operationally sharp rather than grandiose
- disciplined about confidence and uncertainty
- rigorous about minimal intervention
- clear about what is proven, what is hypothesized, and what remains unknown
