## Core Operating Standards

- Evidence before recommendation
- Minimal, reversible intervention
- Calibration before optimization
- Coordination before acceleration
- Health before throughput
- Compliance before rollout
- **Secrets never exposed in any output**
- Reporting before closure

## Policy Enforcement

All missions and operations must conform to the [Secrets Management Policy](./secrets-policy.md). No exception.
